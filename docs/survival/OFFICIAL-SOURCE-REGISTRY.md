# Official Source Registry — PPI Ishikawa Survival Guide

Verified: 2026-09-29  
Purpose: source-of-truth registry for evergreen content under **Hidup di Ishikawa / Survival Guide**.

## Editorial rule

PPI Ishikawa should not become the authority for legal, administrative, medical, safety, tax, or municipal rules.

Use this hierarchy:

1. **Japan-wide rule/system** → national government source.
2. **Prefectural information/service** → Ishikawa Prefecture / IFIE.
3. **Municipal procedure** → the resident's own city/town.
4. **Live/dynamic data** → link to the live official page instead of copying schedules, fees, alerts, or timetables.
5. **PPI practical experience** → clearly label as practical notes/tips, separate from official requirements.

### Content treatment codes

- **R — Rewrite + link:** explain in concise Indonesian, then link to the official source.
- **L — Link/live:** do not duplicate volatile data; give a short explanation and send readers to the live source.
- **P — PPI layer:** add practical notes/checklists based on student experience, while keeping the official source visible.
- **V — Verify first:** useful background, but old or potentially stale; do not copy operational details without newer confirmation.

# Source quality rules

### Tier A — canonical
Current national agency, Ishikawa Prefecture, IFIE, or municipality page directly responsible for the subject. Use as source of truth.

### Tier B — official supporting
Official but indirect, specialist, or data portal source. Use for detail or live lookup.

### Tier C — legacy / verify
Official but clearly old. Keep as background or discovery source only.

Known Tier-C examples in this registry:
- Kahoku Living Guide: guide dated 2017.
- Kahoku foreign-language garbage sheet: page dated 2021.
- Komatsu Living Guide: guide published 2021; portal remains useful but details should be cross-checked.
- Older Kanazawa hazard maps should be linked through their official catalogue and checked against current disaster portals before operational claims.

# Maintenance rule

Every PPI Survival Guide article should contain:

- **Applies to:** Japan / Ishikawa Prefecture / municipality
- **Audience:** student / family / all residents
- **Last verified:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Official source(s):** 1–4 registry IDs
- **PPI practical notes:** clearly separated
- **Volatility:** low / medium / high / live

Recommended review cycle:
- Low: once per year
- Medium: every 6 months
- High: every 3 months
- Live: never mirror the live value; link to the official live source

# Important editorial principle

**Official information tells readers the rules. PPI Ishikawa should tell them what the rules mean in practical student life, in what order to act, what commonly causes confusion, and where to verify the latest official information.**
